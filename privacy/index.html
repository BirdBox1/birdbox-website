Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete your account and everything in it;
  • stop using your data for a particular purpose;
  • hand over your data in a portable form.

Write to info@birdboxcoaching.com. We will respond within one month.

If you subscribe to BirdBox Programming you can also delete your account yourself, at any time, from the Me tab in the app. That cancels your subscription and removes your data straight away.

Where we rely on your consent — health data, marketing, call recordings — you can withdraw it at any time, in the app under Me or by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not undo anything we did while it was in place.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963 — dataprotection.ie. If you live elsewhere in the EU you may complain to your own national authority instead.

Cookies

Essential — always on. What is needed to make the site and the app work: keeping you signed in, remembering your place in a form, and letting Stripe process a payment securely. Without these the service does not function, so they do not require your consent.

Advertising — only if you agree. We run advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and use their pixel to measure whether those adverts work. It tells Meta that a browser visited a page on our site and, where it can, links that to a Meta account.

We ask before it loads. If you decline, it does not run. You can change your mind at any time from the cookie link in the footer.

We do not run analytics that profile you, and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

Changes

We update this notice when what we do changes. The version and date are at the top. Where a change materially affects you we will tell you directly rather than rely on you noticing.
