Why This Seminar Matters

Joint Assessment And Movement Mastery

We emphasise joint movement assessments as a crucial part of your coaching toolkit. They let you validate what you’re seeing, evaluate mobility, and make sure athletes can perform exercises safely — including recognising red flags and staying within your professional scope.

Throughout the weekend we focus on specific joints and how their function affects performance. Starting at the ankle, we explore common limitations and their ripple effects through the kinetic chain. Understanding those connections is what makes better programming and intervention possible.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have completed Level 1?

Yes. BirdBox Level 1 is required before attending Level 2.

Do I receive CEUs?

Yes. You may claim 24 CEUs toward your continuing CrossFit® education on successful completion.

Is the online course extra?

No. The online learning course is included, and you get access before, during and after the live weekend.

What does the weekend focus on?

Movement observation, joint assessment, scope of practice, and a bank of targeted interventions you can use immediately.

Do I need to be a physiotherapist to use the assessments?

No. A core part of the weekend is understanding your scope of practice as a coach, including how to recognise red flags and when to refer on.

Can I split the payment?

Yes. Pay in full, spread it across monthly instalments, or pay a deposit now with the balance two weeks before the course.

There’s no seminar near me — what now?

Email us with your location. We plan the calendar around demand, so it genuinely helps to know where coaches are waiting.
