Study Topics

A Complete Coaching Curriculum

  • Methodology Coaching definitions, coach hierarchies of needs, and shaping your personal coaching philosophy.
  • Know Yourself Understand your personality, traits, and behaviors to improve your coaching effectiveness.
  • Coach-Athlete Relationship Foundational concepts and academic models for building strong relationships.
  • Communication Different types of communication and skills training to improve your coaching practice.
  • Leadership What type of leader/coach you are, and how to improve your behaviors.
  • Biomechanics Principles of biomechanics and how they guide more effective coaching.
  • Dependence, Independence & Interdependence Building athletes who become proficient in their own training.
  • Motivation What makes a coach motivational, and how to become more effective at it.
  • Applicable Coaching Models Major coaching models you can use directly in your own practice.
  • Identification and Interventions Developing your coach's eye and making positive coaching interventions.
  • Psychological Skills Training Interventions to help develop your athletes and your service to them.
  • Conflict Forms of conflict you may face as a coach, and psychological skills to deal with it.
  • Standards, Mastery & Maximum Motor Potential Long-term athlete development and expressing maximum motor potential.
  • Burnout What it is, how to identify and avoid it, and how to support someone experiencing it.
  • Building or Refining Your Own Coaching Philosophy Developing a philosophy that guides you to execute your coaching effectively.
