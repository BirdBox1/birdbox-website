Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prerequisites?

None. The online course is open to coaches at every stage.

How long does it take?

It is self-paced. The English course is expected to take around 60 hours; the translated versions around 40 to 50.

Do I receive CEUs?

Yes. You may claim 44 CEUs toward your continuing CrossFit® education on completion.

What languages is it available in?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese.

Can I split the payment?

Yes. Pay in full, spread it across monthly instalments, or pay a deposit now and the balance before you start.

Is this the same as the live seminar?

It covers the same curriculum at your own pace. The live seminar adds hands-on practice, real-time feedback and written feedback afterwards.

How do I log in after buying?

An email is sent with your learner portal login details. Check your junk folder if it has not arrived within five minutes.
