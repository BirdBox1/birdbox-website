Our Story

We Started BirdBox Because Coaching Standards Needed To Be Better

BirdBox Coaching wasn’t started because the industry needed more courses. It was started because coaching standards were inconsistent. Too many coaches were passionate, but lacked structure. Too many certifications taught information without teaching people how to coach.

Too many athletes were receiving advice based on opinion instead of observation, communication and evidence. We believed coaching education could be better. More practical. More honest. More applicable on Monday morning than impressive on social media.

That Belief Became BirdBox Coaching

Everything we build serves that mission — every seminar, every online course, every piece of written feedback a coach receives after a weekend with us.

Founded By Nathan Bird

Meet The Founder

Nathan Bird founded BirdBox Coaching after two decades of sport and CrossFit® coaching and more than a decade of educating coaches. His background spans rehabilitation, sport psychology and strength and conditioning, including work in sport injury clinics.

“Application without knowledge can be dangerous, but knowledge without application is careless. Coach with reason and purpose, always striving for higher standards.”

— Nathan Bird

That principle sets the standard for everything BirdBox produces. Course content is refined, evaluated and developed by Nathan and the BirdBox team, applying academic knowledge, pedagogy and structured process to every piece of material a coach receives.
