TEC Founding Staff

The Endurance Course launches in 2027. We are looking for experienced endurance coaches who are interested in joining our seminar staff team from the start.

Start your applicationFree to apply · Europe, United States, Asia and Australia

Who we are looking for

Experienced endurance coaches: running, cycling, triathlon, rowing, HYROX and hybrid. You coach athletes week in, week out, you can explain what you see, and you want to help build something new rather than join something finished.

We also want to know about your education. A background in physiology, sport science or strength and conditioning, and your own competition experience, are a real plus.

We are building the team in Europe, the United States, Asia and Australia.

How it works

  1. Apply (free)Send your CV, your coaching philosophy, your education and experience, a written piece and a reference.
  2. Review and callNathan reviews every application himself and invites the coaches he would like to know more about to a call.
  3. Talk it throughWith the coaches who are a good fit, we talk through how they would work with us as TEC launches. What you see of TEC before launch stays confidential.
  4. Coach TECAs TEC launches in your region, coach it with us. Places are by selection, and applying is not a guarantee of a role.

Build it with us.

A small founding team, chosen for experience and the quality of their coaching, across four regions.

Is this you?

We are building the seminar staff team for The Endurance Course. If you are interested and feel you are a good fit, we want to hear from you.

How you would work with us, and where, is something we talk through with each coach.
