1.1What you are buying

Your registration secures one place on the course, seminar or workshop named at checkout, for the named participant. Places are limited and are allocated in order of completed payment.

1.2Price and payment

Prices are shown at checkout in the currency of sale and include VAT where it applies. Where a deposit or payment plan is offered, the remaining balance is charged automatically to the payment method you provide, on the dates shown to you at checkout. If a scheduled payment fails we will contact you, and we may release your place if the balance is not settled.

1.3Transfers and cancellation by you

Because we commit to venues, travel and coaching staff well in advance, course fees are non-refundable. We will instead offer you, at our discretion:

  • a transfer to another date or location of the same course;
  • a credit against a future course, valid for 24 months; or
  • a transfer to the equivalent online course.

To request one of these, contact us at least 14 days before the course start date. Requests inside 14 days are considered but cannot be guaranteed.

You may transfer your place to another person at no charge up to 7 days before the course, provided they meet any prerequisites and complete their own health declaration and waiver.

1.4Discretionary refunds

In exceptional circumstances we may agree to a refund. Where we do, an administration fee of €75 is deducted to cover payment processing, platform and banking costs we cannot recover.

1.5Cancellation or changes by us

We may cancel or reschedule a course — for example because of insufficient registrations, venue failure, coach illness or events outside our control. If we cancel and cannot offer you a suitable alternative date, you will receive a full refund of the course fee.

We are not responsible for your travel, accommodation or other costs. We strongly recommend booking refundable travel or taking out travel insurance.

1.6Your right to cancel (consumers in the EU and UK)

For courses taking place on a specific date, the statutory 14-day cancellation right does not apply. This is the standard exemption for leisure and event services tied to a date.

For online courses, you have 14 days to cancel from purchase. By starting the course within that period you ask us to begin supply immediately and you acknowledge that you lose the right to cancel once you have accessed the content.

1.7Prerequisites

Some courses require you to have completed an earlier level. If you register without the prerequisite we will offer a transfer or credit, but we cannot admit you to the course.

1.8Conduct

We may refuse admission to, or remove from a course, anyone whose behaviour puts themselves or others at risk, or who is under the influence of alcohol or drugs. No refund or credit is due in that case.

03 Health and participation declaration ⌄ Completed after purchase, before you attend. Seen only by your course coaches.

This is not a test and there are no wrong answers. Telling us about an injury or condition does not stop you taking part — it lets your coach give you appropriate alternatives on the day.

  1. Do you have any current injury, pain or physical limitation we should know about?
  2. Have you had surgery or a significant injury in the last 12 months?
  3. Do you have any medical condition that could be affected by strenuous exercise — for example a heart condition, high blood pressure, asthma, epilepsy, or a musculoskeletal, respiratory or circulatory condition?
  4. Are you currently pregnant, or have you given birth in the last 6 months? Yes / no / prefer not to say
  5. Is there anything else you would like your coach to know?
  6. Emergency contact — name, relationship and phone number. Required
  7. Do you carry medication we should know about in an emergency, such as an inhaler or adrenaline auto-injector?

How we handle this. This is health data. We collect it with your explicit consent, show it only to the coaches assigned to your course, use it solely to keep you safe during the Course, and delete it 12 months afterwards. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
