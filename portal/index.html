Coaching philosophy

What you stand for as a coach, and how you want people to experience being coached by you. Seen by the BirdBox team only, never by participants and never on the website.

If you have not written one before, the five steps are a reasonable way in: know your role and your values, decide what you want the people you coach to leave with, set the standards you hold yourself to as well as them, work out how you will know it is working, and choose the leadership style you want to grow into.
