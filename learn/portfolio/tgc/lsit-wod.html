My L-sit workout of the day

Plan a workout with L-sits for your best athlete, then scale it so every athlete in the room gets the same stimulus in a position they can hold with mastery. There is no "Rx" in gymnastics: the aim is position and perfection, not just getting through the time.

1. My workout

Write the workout as you would put it on the board for your best athlete.

2. The stimulus

What is this workout for? Mastery comes first, then volume, then intensity. A training workout is not a competition workout: not every day is a test.

3. Scaling options

Most of your class will need to scale to hold the shape with virtuosity. First mark the position your workout is written for, then choose the easier positions you will offer. Every one keeps the same key points.

4. The shape we will not lose

Tick the key points every athlete must keep at their level. If they cannot keep them, they scale.

5. After the session

Did the scaling keep the stimulus for everyone? Who is ready to move up a level, or needs to move down?
