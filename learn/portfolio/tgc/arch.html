The arch: length and tension

The pelvis does not change in the arch: the bottom stays squeezed in a posterior pelvic tilt, and the shape comes from extension of the thoracic spine, with the abs in tight to protect the lower back. Switch the tension on and off, rise into the arch, and watch the length from fingertips to toes.

My athlete's spine

Not every athlete rises to the same height, and it is not only tight muscles or strength. The shape and size of each athlete's spinous processes and facet joints decide how far the spine can extend. With less interspinous space, or a facet that restricts extension, the spinous processes meet sooner and the arch stops there.

A longer torso with larger vertebrae often has more space between them. Move the arch and the spine above, and watch the spinous processes come together. Where bone meets bone, more mobility or strength work will not change it: coach length and tension, not height.
