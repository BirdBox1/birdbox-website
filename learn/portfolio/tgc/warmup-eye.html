Warm-up coaches eye

A warm-up gives you an assessment of every athlete without being invasive. Run a PVC pipe warm-up or your standardised gymnastics warm-up, record the faults you see, then follow one athlete: where will that fault show up in their gymnastics, and what will you do about it?

2. Faults I saw

For each fault, write how many athletes showed it, and what you noticed. Leave it at 0 if you did not see it. If you filmed the warm-up, add the video and watch it as you go (optional).

3. One athlete

Pick one athlete and one fault from today. Use initials or a first name only.
