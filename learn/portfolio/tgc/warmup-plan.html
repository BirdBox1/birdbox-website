My standardised warm-up

Frequent exposure to gymnastics shapes is the most effective way of developing positions. Build the standardised gymnastics warm-up you will use with your athletes, structured with R.A.M.P, and plan what you will look for while they move.

1. My warm-up, structured with R.A.M.P

List the movements and how long each part takes. Remember that mobilise and potentiate can carry on later in the session.

2. My standardised line drills

Tick the drills you will use every session. For each one, write what you will look for and where you will stand to see it.

3. Planes of motion

Most benchmark workouts and strength work move athletes in the sagittal plane. Rotating at speed under load in a workout can be dangerous, so the warm-up is a safe place to train rotation.

4. When I would use D.R.O.M instead

A dynamic range of motion warm-up suits sessions where movement positions are the main objective.

5. My reflections

What do you currently do, and not do, in your warm-ups? What changes would you like to make to your coaching practice or your own warm-up?
