Head path review — bar muscle-up

Film a bar muscle-up from the side, trace the athlete's head path, then write a critical review: where does the head path stray, why, and what would you coach next?

How it works

  1. Film from the side, level with the bar, with the whole athlete in shot. A phone held still is enough.
  2. Choose the video. When you save, only the rep you mark is kept, privately, in your portfolio.
  3. Mark one rep. Start: hanging on the bar in the arch. End: the catch on top of the bar at the end of the first movement — if the athlete dips out at the top, end before the dip.
  4. Tap the bar, and tap the athlete's ear at the start and end of the rep so the tracker knows who to follow. The head path follows the ear.
  5. Trace the head path. Check the line follows the ear — switch on “Edit points” and drag any point that is not on the ear.
  6. Write your review using the analysis and the prompts, then save it to your portfolio.

Analysis

The analysis looks at the rep you marked, from the arch to the catch on top. How far the arch sits from the bar does not matter; what matters is that the head keeps a tall, smooth arch and does not divert sharply off it. The bands are a first guide while we calibrate them, not a pass mark.
