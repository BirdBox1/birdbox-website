Programming case studies

Four real-world programmes, each with a problem in it. Your job is to spot it, fix it, and explain why, using what you learned in this unit.

Why case studies

Knowing the rules is the start. The skill is seeing when a real programme breaks them, for a real person, and knowing what to do about it.

What you get from it

  • Practice using the risks, rules and programming aspects

  • A record of how you programme, as a PDF

How to do it

1Read

The question first, then the case in full.

2Notice

The risks, the rules it breaks, and the numbers.

3Programme

Your plan, and the parts of the unit you used.

4Open

Nathan's answer unlocks. Yours is kept as written.

5Check

Got it, partly or missed, then reflect.

6Apply

The same thinking for your own gym.

About 20 minutes a case, in any order. Everything saves as you go.
