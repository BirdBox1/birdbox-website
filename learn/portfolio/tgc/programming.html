My gym's programming

Read a week of your gym's programming to see its volume, intensity and frequency, then build one to three gymnastics accessory sessions on top of it, and see the whole week together.

1My gym's week

Add screenshots of the week (from your gym's app, a spreadsheet or a photo of the whiteboard), or paste it in. The week is read and filled in for you: then check it, and fix anything that is not right.

2What the week shows

Volume is how much (reps, load, distance), frequency how many days a pattern is trained, and intensity how hard (the % of a maximum, or how the workout is run).

LowModerateHighA = my accessory session

3My accessory sessions

Gymnastics accessory coaching on top of the gym's week (programming rule 1: a supplement that does not take away the day's main element). Choose how many, put each on a day, and add the drills by hand or from a screenshot. For holds put the seconds held; for slow lowerings the seconds of lowering.

4The whole week

The gym's week and my accessory sessions together. Each accessory session is checked against the unit: about 120 seconds of isometric work as a training target, and no more than 125 seconds of purposeful eccentric loading in a session.
