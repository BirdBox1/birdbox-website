Pelvis and rib cage: the four-way complex

The pelvis tilts anteriorly or posteriorly, and the rib cage opens or comes down. Move them below and watch what happens to the shape, standing and on the floor. Posterior pelvic tilt with the rib cage down is your hollow: a straight line. Anterior pelvic tilt with the rib cage open is your squat shape.
