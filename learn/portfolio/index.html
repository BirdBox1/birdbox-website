Self-perception spider diagram

A simple way to see where your view of your coaching matches how others experience it — and where it does not.

How it works

  1. Your qualities. Think of around 5 traits that make an excellent coach, then build them out to 10–15. Find the words yourself — they represent what you think good coaching is.
  2. Rate yourself from 1 to 10 on each one.
  3. Ask a colleague (for example your lead coach) and your athletes to rate you too. Send them a link below and their scores fill in by themselves — or type in scores you collected in person.
  4. Look at the gaps. Where others see you differently from how you see yourself is where the learning is.

    My reactions in coaching

    Reactions are fast and often automatic. Behaviours are chosen. This task helps you see which is which in your coaching.

    How it works

    1. Spend 5–10 minutes listing things your members do that annoy you, please you and sadden you.
    2. For each one, write down how you react — what you say, do, or how you look.
    3. Decide: is that reaction considered and purposeful, or something you are not proud of?
    4. For any reaction you do not like, write the behaviour you would rather provide instead.

    What I want to change

      My reflections

      Do you see any patterns? Which situations trigger your least considered reactions? What will you practise in your next session?

      Video review — non-verbal communication

      You cannot expect instant changes. This task builds new habits over 2–4 weeks — film, review, practise one thing, then film again.

      How it works

      1. Film some of your coaching. A phone propped up in the corner is enough.
      2. Watch it once for each area below — only look at that one area each time.
      3. Note the good things first, then where you can improve, and score each area.
      4. Make an action plan for your weakest area and focus on it for a week of coaching. Give yourself 2–4 weeks.
      5. Film again, review it the same way, and see how you have changed. Ask a mentor or peer coach for feedback if you can.
