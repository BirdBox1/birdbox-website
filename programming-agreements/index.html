In this document, “we”, “us” and “BirdBox” mean BirdBox Coaching Limited, its directors, employees, coaches, contractors and representatives. “Your programming” means any training we write, publish or send to you, and any feedback, review or advice a coach gives you.

Your coach writes your programming and reviews footage you send. Your coach is not present when you train. They cannot see your surroundings, your equipment, your technique except in the clips you choose to send, or how you feel on the day.

This is not medical advice, physiotherapy, diagnosis or treatment. Nothing your coach says replaces advice from a doctor or a qualified medical professional.

Your programming involves physical exercise, including gymnastics, weightlifting and other functional fitness movements, using equipment such as barbells, rings, bars, ropes and boxes.

I understand and accept that this carries a genuine risk of injury, including:

I understand that these risks cannot be eliminated, that not every risk can be listed, and that risk may arise from my own actions, the condition of the venue or equipment I choose to train with, or from a coach misjudging my ability or condition from the limited picture they have of it.

2.3 I am responsible for where and how I train

I confirm that:

  • I train in a space that is safe for the movements I am doing, with equipment in sound condition, and I inspect it before I use it;

I understand that programming is written in advance and cannot account for how I slept, what hurts today, or what has changed since it was written. I will use my own judgement, scale or skip movements as needed, and tell my coach when I do.

03

Health and participation declaration

Completed before your programming starts, and confirmed every 12 months. Seen only by your coach and by BirdBox administrators.

This is not a test and there are no wrong answers. Telling us about an injury or condition does not stop you training with us — it lets your coach programme appropriately for you.

  1. Do you have any current injury, pain or physical limitation we should know about?
  2. Have you had surgery or a significant injury in the last 12 months?
  3. Has a doctor ever told you that you have a heart condition, or that you should only exercise under medical supervision?
  4. Do you get chest pain or shortness of breath when you exercise, or have you had chest pain in the past month when you were not exercising?
  5. Do you lose your balance from dizziness, or ever lose consciousness?
  6. Do you have any other medical condition that could be affected by strenuous exercise — for example high blood pressure, asthma, epilepsy, diabetes, or a musculoskeletal, respiratory or circulatory condition?
  7. Are you currently pregnant, or have you given birth in the last 6 months?
  8. Do you take any medication we should know about, or carry medication for an emergency such as an inhaler or adrenaline auto-injector?
  9. Has a doctor or physiotherapist told you to avoid particular movements or types of training?
  10. Is there anything else you would like your coach to know?

We also ask for an emergency contact — name, relationship and phone number — and whether you usually train alone, which changes how your coach programmes heavy overhead and inverted work.

If you answer yes to questions 3, 4, 5 or 7 Please speak to your doctor before you start, and let your coach know what they say. Your coach will hold off writing your programming until you confirm you have been cleared. You can still use the app, the community and the calls in the meantime, and your subscription is not affected.

How we handle this

This is health data. We collect it with your explicit consent, show it only to the coach assigned to you and to BirdBox administrators, and use it solely to programme safely for you.

We ask you to confirm it is still accurate every 12 months. If you do not, your access continues as normal but your coach is told the declaration is out of date, and they may ask you before writing your next block. We delete it 12 months after you leave, and sooner if you ask.

I confirm this information is accurate, and I will tell my coach if anything changes.

04

Your training footage

How your clips, photos and calls are used. Some of this is part of the service; the rest is your choice.

4.1 Coaching — part of the service

When you upload a clip for feedback, your coach watches it and may record a video reply containing your footage with drawing and commentary over it. That reply goes to you.

Your footage and your coach’s replies are visible to you, to the coach assigned to you, and to BirdBox administrators. They are not shown to other athletes.

This is the service you are paying for, so it is not optional — but it stays within your coaching relationship.

4.2 The community — your choice

If you post a clip or photo in the community, other athletes on your programme can see it. Nothing is ever moved into the community from your private feedback; only what you choose to post there.

I understand that what I post in the community is visible to other athletes on my programme.

4.3 Group calls — your choice

Group calls are recorded. The recording is available afterwards to everyone on your programme, including your camera and anything you say.

You may join with your camera off — you will not be asked to explain why — and you can watch the recording instead of attending.

I understand group calls are recorded and shared with others on my programme.

4.4 Teaching within the coaching team — optional

I am happy for my footage to be shown to other BirdBox coaches for teaching and coach development. Never published, never outside BirdBox.

4.5 Marketing — optional

I am happy for BirdBox to use my footage or photographs in marketing — social media, the website, course materials.
  • You will not be paid for this.
  • We will not use your footage in a way that is misleading or damaging to you.
  • We will not publish your full name alongside your footage unless you agree separately.
  • Before we use any particular clip we will ask you about that clip. This tick is your general agreement; it is not a blank cheque.

4.6 Changing your mind

You can change any of the optional answers above at any time in the app, under Me, or by emailing info@birdboxcoaching.com. From then on we stop the new use, and we remove material from our own channels where we reasonably can.

We may not be able to recall material already printed, already shared by others, or a recording someone has already downloaded.

4.7 How long we keep it

  • Your training footage: while you are with us, and for 12 months after you leave. Then deleted.
  • Group call recordings: 3 months, then deleted.
  • Footage you have agreed we may use in marketing: kept separately for as long as that material is in use, and deleted when you withdraw consent.

You can ask us to delete any of your footage at any time.

I have read and understood how my footage is used.
