Inside the Seminar

What Two Days Actually Look Like

Gymnastics is purely bodyweight movement, and the human body is infinitely complex. Add task completion or a time priority and it can be overwhelming. We'll teach you to understand it differently.

Understanding Gymnastics Movement

There is no greater modality than gymnastics for affecting change on a population. Its essence is coordination — recruiting the right musculature for the right task. Simple in concept, increasingly complex as movement becomes compound.

Movement Assessment

We'll teach you to see and correct movement more simply, so you can assess what's in front of you and act on it rather than guess.

How to Spot and Self-Spot

The goal of any coach is to progress an athlete as quickly and safely as possible. There is no faster route to gymnastic success than spotting and self-spotting.

Programming and Scaling

Gymnastics programming doesn't need to be hard. It just needs to be often. We'll teach you to program gymnastics regularly to support skill development and responsible intensity.

Class Management

Product delivery is key — in the training world you're only as good as your last class. We'll help you build the ideal class plan while productively implementing gymnastics exposure.

Seminar Format

Lecture, Demo, Practice, Feedback

Our two-day seminar blends lecture, demonstration, practice, feedback, and collaborative drills. You'll leave with a coaching and training model you can apply on Monday morning.
