Overview

Built To Complement, Not Replace

Rather than replacing your current programming, TGC provides targeted gymnastics training to help develop the strength, shapes, body awareness and movement competency that are often difficult to build through regular class programming alone.

Built by The Gymnastics Course coaching team, the programme uses the same core methodology and coaching principles taught throughout our Level 1 seminar. Combining years of coaching experience across CrossFit®, gymnastics and strength and conditioning, it follows a progression-based approach to long-term athletic development.

Each training block is scalable across a wide range of abilities, whether you’re working toward your first strict pull-up and handstand or refining higher-level skills for competition. Expect structured progressions, clear coaching intent, and programming that prioritises movement quality, technical understanding and sustainable progress.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gymnastics experience?

None. Sessions are designed with scalable progressions suitable for a wide range of ability levels.

Will this replace my current programming?

No. TGC Programming is built to complement your existing CrossFit® training, not replace it.

Where do I train?

Any CrossFit® gym or functional fitness facility with a pull-up bar, rings, a plyo box and floor space.

What if I don’t have all the kit?

We occasionally use additional equipment such as parallettes. Those sessions can be adapted if you don’t have access.

How is it delivered?

Through the athlete app, with your programming, movement demonstrations and coaching notes in one place.

Can I cancel?

All tiers are monthly, so you can stop when it no longer suits your training.
