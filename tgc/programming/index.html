Overview

Built To Complement, Not Replace

Rather than replacing your current programming, TGC provides targeted gymnastics training to help develop the strength, shapes, body awareness and movement competency that are often difficult to build through regular class programming alone.

Built by The Gymnastics Course coaching team, the programme uses the same core methodology and coaching principles taught throughout our Level 1 seminar. Combining years of coaching experience across CrossFit®, gymnastics and strength and conditioning, every session has a clear purpose.

Training is scalable across a wide range of abilities, whether you’re working toward your first strict pull-up and handstand or refining higher-level skills for competition. Expect structured progressions, clear coaching intent, and sessions that fit around the rest of your training.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gymnastics experience?

None. Sessions are designed with scalable progressions suitable for a wide range of ability levels, and you pick your level each day.

Will this replace my current programming?

No. TGC Programming is built to complement your existing CrossFit® training, not replace it.

How does the free trial work?

Two weeks of TGC programming in the app, with no card asked for. Nothing is charged when it ends. If you would like to carry on, choose a plan.

What is the difference between the plans?

Tier 1 is the full gymnastics programming. Tier 2 adds the premium community, where you can post your videos, and the weekly group call. Tier 3 adds written and video feedback from your own coach on your training and videos.

Tiers 4 and 5 are one-to-one. Instead of the shared gymnastics programming, your coach writes a bespoke programme for you, with a 1:1 call and written and video feedback in the app. Tier 4 is skill only: a gymnastics programme built around the skills you are working on, alongside your own training. Tier 5 is your full CrossFit programme, written around you. Places are limited, so you apply first.

What is a block?

A set number of weeks on one skill, such as handstands or strict toes to bar, written by a TGC coach. You pay once and choose the Monday you start, or start today. Coach feedback on your videos can be added; with feedback you can pay in full or every 4 weeks.

Which currency will I pay in?

Your own: pounds in the UK, euros in Europe, Australian dollars in Australia, Canadian dollars in Canada and US dollars everywhere else. Prices include VAT. If we have guessed your country wrong, use Change country above the plans.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

In BirdBox Learn, under Programming: change plan, update your card or cancel. Cancelling stops the next payment, and your programming runs to the end of the month you have paid for.

Where are my receipts?

Every receipt and invoice is in BirdBox Learn, under Receipts, with the same email you use in the app.

Where do I train?

Any CrossFit® gym or functional fitness facility with a pull-up bar, rings, a plyo box and floor space.
