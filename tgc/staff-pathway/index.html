TGC Staff Pathway

A year of mentorship with BirdBox staff for coaches who want to develop towards working with TGC: on our seminars, in our mentorship or on our programming.

Start your applicationFree to apply · five places in the first intake

Who it is for

Coaches anywhere in the world who have completed TGC, can perform the movements we teach to a high standard, and want to develop as a coach, not only as an athlete. The first intake has five places.

Where we bring in new staff depends on where our courses are growing. If we are not taking on staff in your region yet, the mentorship year still develops you, and you are part of the group we recruit from when we are.

How it works

  1. Apply (free)Send a video of your coaching, a video of each TGC movement, your CV, your coaching philosophy, a written piece and a reference.
  2. AssessmentNathan reviews every application himself, and may invite you to a call. If you are not accepted this time, you get feedback and a plan of courses and study to strengthen your next application. You can apply again after six months.
  3. The mentorship yearTwelve months of one-to-one and group mentorship, video review of your coaching and guided study for your tracks.
  4. Intern, assist, leadAs you progress, you may be brought in as an intern on a TGC course. You shadow an assistant coach and learn that role directly. Your travel and accommodation are covered. Staff roles are offered by selection as they open.

The people we bring in as staff come from this group.

As roles open, we select from this group on the coaching and progress you show through the year. A place on the pathway is the route in, not a guarantee of a role.

Each month

  • A one-to-one call with your BirdBox mentor
  • A group call with your intake, at a time that works across time zones
  • Video review of your coaching
  • Set study for your tracks, with guidance, followed up in your calls

The pathway is a 12-month commitment: once you start, the full year is completed. It is an opportunity to develop and be seen by our team. It is not an offer of work. You only pay if you are accepted.
