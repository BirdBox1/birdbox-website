Overview

Working Towards Virtuosity

Level 2 expands on TGC Level 1, allowing coaches and athletes to see, attempt, and spot next-level gymnastics skills — and to understand why our training processes are best practice.

It's an opportunity to rediscover the fundamentals of CrossFit gymnastic movements, working towards virtuosity and mastery as you seek higher skills, with a strong emphasis on learning safely and becoming a confident spotter.

Inside the Level 2 Seminar

What's Covered

  • Floor Work — landing, forward and backward rolls, diving rolls, conditioning shapes, backward extension roll
  • Ring Work — inversion, stability and pushing tall, back lever, front lever, backward and forward rolls, dynamic swings, routines, iron cross, front kip
  • Parallel Bars — support, dips, dip swings, dynamic swinging, upstart, hanging in L-sit, swing to handstand, travelling, dismounting
  • Bar Work — bar muscle-ups, pull-over, back hip circle, the kip swing, glide kip, baby giants, bar complexes, and more advanced combinations
  • Ropes & Pegs — coaching new apparatus appearing in the CrossFit® environment, including handstand walking obstacles, peg boards, and rope climbs
  • Stretching — principles, research, and current practices in developing the length-tension relationship
  • Programming — applying Level 2 movement into individual, group, recreational, athlete, and box programming
