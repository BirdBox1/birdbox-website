Who Is This For?

Ready to Go Beyond the Basics

This course is for coaches and athletes who have completed TGC Level 1 and want to advance their movement knowledge. Whether you’re helping others break through plateaus or chasing higher skills yourself, Level 2 is where the detail lives.

The Problem

Most People Stop at Basics

Level 1 builds your foundation. But whether you’re coaching or performing advanced skills — bar muscle-ups, pistols, handstand push-ups, and dynamic transitions — the gap between competent and confident is where most people stall.

What You’ll Develop in Level 2

Beyond the Fundamentals

Sharper Movement Analysis

Refine your ability to spot subtle faults, timing errors, and compensations — in yourself and others.

Higher-Level Skills

Develop advanced variations of toes-to-bar, pistols, muscle-ups, inversions, and dynamic transitions.

Better Progress Planning

Build smarter, more effective training and coaching progressions to keep advanced skills moving forward.

Overview

Working Towards Virtuosity

Level 2 expands on TGC Level 1, allowing coaches and athletes to see, attempt, and spot next-level gymnastics skills — and to understand why our training processes are best practice. It’s an opportunity to rediscover the fundamentals of CrossFit® gymnastics movements, working towards virtuosity and mastery as you seek higher skills. Learning in a safe environment matters, so the emphasis is on becoming proficient at the movements and a confident spotter.

What’s Covered

Floor Work

Landing, forward and backward rolls, diving rolls, conditioning shapes, backward extension roll.

Ring Work

Inversion, stability and pushing tall, back lever, front lever, backward and forward rolls, dynamic swings, routines, iron cross, front kip.

Parallel Bars

Support, dips, dip swings, dynamic swinging, upstart, hanging in L-sit, swing to handstand, travelling, dismounting.

Bar Work

Bar muscle-ups, pull-over, back hip circle, the kip swing, glide kip, baby giants, bar complexes, ice cream scoops, levers and butter makers.

Ropes & Pegs

Coaching new apparatus appearing in the CrossFit® environment, including handstand walking obstacles, peg boards, and rope climbs.

Stretching

Principles, research, and current practices in developing the length-tension relationship.

Programming

Applying Level 2 movement into individual, group, recreational, athlete, and box programming.

Course Objectives

What the Weekend Is Built To Do

Understand gymnastics movements beyond the current Rx standard
Apply new movements to training programmes for strength development
Develop coaching tools and athletic principles for floor, rings, high bar, parallel bars, ropes and peg boards
Understand the length-tension relationship and how to stretch
Understand how to coach, spot and scale the future of CrossFit® movements

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have completed Level 1?

Yes. TGC Level 1 is required before attending Level 2.

Do I need to be able to do every movement?

No. The recommended movements help athletes get the most from the practical work, but you do not need to perform a movement to coach it effectively. Coaches are welcome at any ability, and you will have spotting throughout.

Do I receive CEUs?

Yes. You may claim 14 CEUs toward your continuing CrossFit® education upon successful completion.

What language is the seminar in?

Language varies by location and is stated on each seminar listing. Check the course page before you register.

Can I pay a deposit?

Yes. A deposit secures your place, with the balance collected before the course.

Are there discounts for Level 2?

Yes — the same as Level 1 for first responders, full-time school teachers, active duty or veteran military personnel, and groups of five or more at 25%. The one exception is the 50% re-attendance discount, which does not apply to Level 2.

What should I bring?

Your registration confirmation, training clothes, and food and fluids for the day.
