Clear Progressions

Coaching Interventions

What You’ll Learn

Four Things That Change How You Coach

Understanding Gymnastics Movement

Gymnastics is purely bodyweight movement, and the human body is infinitely complex. We will teach you to understand it differently.

Movement Assessment

The essence of gymnastics is coordination — recruiting the proper musculature for the right task. Simple in concept, increasingly complex as movement becomes compound. We will teach you to see and correct movement more simply.

How to Spot and Self-Spot

Any coach's goal is to help an athlete progress as quickly and safely as possible. There is no faster route to gymnastic success than spotting and self-spotting.

Programming and Scaling

Gymnastics programming doesn't need to be hard. It just needs to be often. We will teach you to program gymnastics regularly to support skill development and responsible intensity.

Who Should Attend?

Built for Coaches and Athletes

Whether you’re a CrossFit coach, part-time trainer, gym owner, or competitive athlete, this seminar is built for you. If you want a rock-solid understanding of gymnastics and how to coach it, you belong in the room.

Topics Covered

Gymnastics Coaching Methodology & Hierarchy of Development

Methodology & Rules

The coaching methodology, rules, and laws that govern safe, effective gymnastics training.

Warm-Ups & Movement Assessment

Warm-up design, identifying movement limitations, coaching approaches, and training interventions.

Foundational Shapes

Hollow, arch, hollow rocks, arch rocks, L-sits, V-sits, pelvis tilt and ribcage control.

Hanging & Grip

Hang in hollow, lat compression, and grip development.

Pulling, From First Rep to Mastery

Strict pull-up and toes-to-bar progressions through beat swings, kipping pull-ups, kipping toes-to-bar, butterfly pull-ups, pull-overs, and bar muscle-ups.

Squatting & Pressing

Air squat biomechanics and coaching approaches, pistols, and push-ups.

Rings

Ring push-ups and low ring training accessories, strict ring muscle-ups, dynamic ring swings, and kipping ring muscle-ups.

Inversions

Headstand, handstand, and strict and kipping handstand push-ups.

Coaching Mindset

Coaching styles and a growth mindset toward training and coaching gymnastics.

Programming

A programming lecture and clear next steps for training and coaching.

No. Level 1 is designed for coaches and athletes at any stage.

You don't need to be an athlete — just someone who wants to understand and coach movement better.

No. Many coaches from other sports attend and benefit.

Yes. You may claim 14 CEUs toward your continuing CrossFit® education upon successful completion.

What language is the seminar in?

Can I attend alone?

Absolutely. Most coaches do, and you will feel welcome.

Can I split the payment?
