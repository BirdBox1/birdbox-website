The Problem

Still Coaching Without a System?

Most coaches feel unsure when teaching gymnastics. You've relied on Instagram clips, old drills, or trial and error. This seminar changes that.

What You'll Get at the Seminar

A Real Coaching System

01
Movement Diagnostics
Learn how to identify faults in bodyweight movement and coach them effectively.
02
Clear Progressions
Master the "static before dynamic, strict before kipping" framework with real-life coaching applications.
03
Coaching Interventions
Use cues, constraints, and interventions to create meaningful movement change on the spot.
Who Should Attend?

Built for Coaches and Athletes

Whether you're a CrossFit coach, part-time trainer, gym owner, or competitive athlete, this seminar is built for you. If you want a rock-solid understanding of movement, this is where it begins.

Topics Covered

Gymnastics Coaching Methodology & Hierarchy of Development

  • How to teach hollow, arch, and foundational shapes
  • The role of strict strength and isometric control
  • Diagnosing faults and applying real-time fixes
  • Pull-up, toes-to-bar and inversion progressions
  • Pistols, push-ups, ring and bar muscle-ups
  • Warm-up design using RAMP and DROM frameworks
  • Teaching with clarity, intent, and confidence
Seminar Format

Lecture, Demo, Practice, Feedback

Our two-day seminar is a blend of lecture, demo, practice, feedback, and collaborative drills. You'll leave with a coaching and training model you can apply on Monday morning.
