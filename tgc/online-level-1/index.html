Overview

Certification Requirements

The Gymnastics Course Online is designed for CrossFit® coaches, strength and conditioning coaches, personal trainers, clinical practitioners and athletes — enhancing movement understanding, training practices, and coaching approaches.

The course includes short knowledge checks at the end of each unit and is to be completed in order. Once finished, a thirty-question multiple-choice exam awards certification, requiring 80% or higher to pass. This course awards 35 CrossFit® CEUs.
