Overview

Certification Requirements

The Gymnastics Course Online is designed for CrossFit® coaches, strength and conditioning coaches, personal trainers, clinical practitioners and athletes. The course includes short knowledge checks at the end of each unit and is completed in order. Once finished, a thirty-question multiple-choice exam awards certification for the merit of “TGC L1 Trainer”.
