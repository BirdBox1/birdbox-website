Questions

Before you pre-register

Do I pay anything now?

No. We save your card to hold your place for January. Nothing is charged until your discovery call is booked and confirmed. Then the €150 call fee is taken.

When does it start?

Mentorship starts on Monday 4 January 2027. Discovery calls take place before then, so you're ready to go in the first week.

What happens on the discovery call?

60 to 90 minutes with your mentor about you, your coaching and where you want to go. The call is recorded so your mentor can write your Discovery Report properly. You keep the report whether you continue or not.

Can I choose my mentor?

Yes, you can pick a preferred mentor. We'll do our best, but if a mentor's places are full we'll pair you with someone else, in a time zone that works for you.

Do I need to have done a BirdBox course?

No. Mentorship is for any coach who wants to get better: class coaches, head coaches and gym owners.

How long is it, and can I stop?

There's no fixed length. You pay every 4 weeks and can cancel any time from your BirdBox account.

I'm a gym owner. Can I get a business invoice?

Yes. Add your business name and VAT number when you pre-register. An EU business outside Ireland with a valid VAT number isn't charged VAT, and every invoice shows your details.
